Inbox & AI ISA

Every channel lands in one list. The hot ones rise by themselves.

Email, SMS, Messenger, Instagram and calls arrive into one stream. Triage labels each message by intent and the list re-sorts: hot first, then warm, then everything that can wait.

  • Intents: hot, warm, not now, wrong number, stop, angry. STOP is caught by rules and flags DNC instantly
  • The AI ISA answers in seconds, asks one question at a time, hands off with a call task when the seller is hot
  • Three AI draft styles, thread summary, Spanish translation, SMS deliverability coach

  • Actions: SMS, email, task, note, stage, owner round-robin, segment, AI caller
