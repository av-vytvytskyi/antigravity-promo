An ISA that answers at 9pm, asks one question at a time, and knows when to hand off.

Write the goal once: which facts to collect and when a human should take over. The ISA extracts facts from each reply, asks for the next missing one, and stops the moment the seller is hot, asks for a person, or agrees to a call.

  • Channels: SMS, email, Messenger, and missed call → text-back
  • Single-reply mode for a canned answer, conversation mode for the AI agent with a reply cap
  • Handoff flips intent to hot, creates the deal, fills qualification fields and books a call task with everything collected
  • Filters: sender domain, fire only after N unanswered inbound messages
  • Simulator: run the rule against a fake conversation before it talks to anyone

Your list sorts itself by who is about to sell.

Every inbound message gets an intent. Priority mode puts hot first, angry second, then warm, and inside each group the person who has waited longest. Filters by intent, Needs Reply, Unread, Mine, Starred, Later and Scheduled.

  • Contact context panel beside every thread: CRM match, deals, stage, asking price, motivation, urgency, notes
  • Assign a conversation to a teammate. See who is viewing or typing on the same thread in real time
  • Snooze with cancel-on-reply, so the thread wakes early when the lead answers
  • Scheduled send for SMS and email, and a best-send-time suggestion per contact

When you reply yourself, the AI hands you a head start.

AI draft, three ways

Short and direct, warm and detailed, or negotiator. Written as an acquisitions rep talking to a motivated seller, not as a chatbot.

Catch me up

A long thread condensed to what was agreed and the next step, including the latest AI call summaries from the linked deal.

Translate

Spanish in, English out, and your reply back into Spanish in the same casual SMS tone. A looks-Spanish heuristic offers it automatically.

SMS deliverability coach

Scores a text 0–100 against US A2P 10DLC rules: spam words, links, caps, length, opt-out tone. Suggests an improved version.

Extract qualification

Pulls asking price, motivation, urgency and address out of a conversation and writes them onto the deal.

Summary → task

One click turns a summary into a fully prefilled task: type, title, priority, due date, contact and deal.

Rules you can read. A simulator you can trust.

Each autoreply rule is a card: channel, mode, instructions, actions after the reply. Switch it on, watch the reply counter, switch it off. Test any change against a fake thread first.

  • Templates for email and SMS with merge fields: first name, property address, city, deal stage
  • Share templates with the team or keep them private
  • Email open and click tracking with a per-message timeline
