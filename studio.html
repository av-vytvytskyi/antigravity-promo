Creative Studio & Web

Creatives, copy, pages and domains. Made in minutes, not ordered from a freelancer.

Pick an idea, a style and a person setting. The studio generates the square, resizes it for story, portrait and landscape, writes the headline and primary text variations, and files everything in one library record ready for Meta or Google.

Ideas first. Then images that match your ICP’s actual pain.

The studio reads your saved business context and audience profiles. Ask for general concepts or one creative per pain, pick the idea, and it becomes a generation with headline overlay, angle and copy already decided.

  • Style reference images with a match-strength control, creativity slider from strict to creative
  • Copy packs: several headline and primary-text variations per image, structured for RSA and Meta limits
  • Upload your own photo and let the AI smart-resize it into every ad format
  • AI edit an existing image with a sentence. Generate copy from any image with Claude Vision
app.antigravity.io/ad-creative/create

Landing pages with real-estate blocks, published on your domain.

A drag-and-drop page builder with a curated block library: cash-offer heroes, distressed-property sections, foreclosure and probate pages, testimonials, state pages, forms. Publish to a public URL, or buy a domain from your project balance and point it there automatically.

  • Desktop, laptop, tablet and mobile previews, grid and flex layout controls
  • AI-suggested domain names from your business context, auto-renew, DNS configured for you
  • One-line tracking script installs every pixel and tag you own on the new page
app.antigravity.io/tools/website

Your brand, extracted from your website in two minutes.

Paste a URL. The analyzer pulls your logo, colors, typography, description and products into Project Settings. From then on creatives, domain suggestions and the AI’s tone use your brand, not a generic one.

  • Products catalog with features and benefits, analyzed from the site
  • Audience profiles: five ICPs generated fast, then enriched one by one, editable
  • Brand tone and excluded words respected in every generated text
