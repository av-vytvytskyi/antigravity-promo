A weekly read from a strategist, not a dashboard.

Insights judges each campaign only against its own objective. Leads are not sales, traffic is not awareness. Cost per result is compared to the median of its own group, never to a made-up benchmark.

  • Sections in the order you act: Scale, Creatives, Audience, Fix
  • Every item names its scope: “consistent across 5 of 6 Meta lead campaigns”
  • Technical reason in brackets, so your media buyer can verify it
  • Ads Copilot answers questions over the same data and never invents a number
app.antigravity.io/ads/insights

Cost per deal, not cost per click.

Each lead keeps the campaign and ad it came from. When a deal closes you enter the assignment fee on the card, and Insights attributes spend, contracts and fees back to the campaign, creative and platform.

  • Revenue by campaign, matched through UTM attribution on contacts and deals
  • Budget guards: pause or create a task when results stop, cost per result climbs, CPC rises or frequency signals fatigue
  • Native Meta and Google recommendations pulled in, with Apply and Dismiss where it is safe
  • Entity drawer with a 28-day daily series, synced twice a day, last 3 days rewritten as attribution lands

One budget. Both platforms. Launched paused, so you review first.

The Smart Campaign wizard splits a daily budget across Meta and Google by your priority, resolves one location set to both platforms’ geo targets, picks a pixel event and a conversion goal, and creates everything paused. One button flips it all live.

  • Pace: Calm, Standard or Express. Priority: Cheapest lead, Balanced or Quality
  • Live mockups in Facebook feed, Instagram story, Google Search, Discover and YouTube
  • Remember locations for next time, reuse creatives from the library

An agency-grade account audit, any time you want one.

Tracking, targeting, ads and landing pages, account structure, budget and bidding. Each check returns Pass, Evaluate or Fail with a plain-English reason, the offending items and a one-click fix where it is safe to automate.

  • Primary conversion actions, attribution model, funnel coverage, UTM tags on active ads
  • Presence-only location targeting, exclusion lists, negative keywords, audience adoption
  • Overall score and a score per area, re-run after every fix
app.antigravity.io/ads/audit

One script installs every pixel and tag you own.

Tracking lists your Meta pixels, observed events and custom conversions, your Google AW tags and conversion goals, and the creatives running on both platforms. A public loader at /t/{token}.js installs all of them on any site in one line.

  • Copy the canonical Meta Pixel base code or the gtag snippet per tag
  • Active creatives with last-30-day stats and previews
  • Ads Reports: every ad flattened with creative thumbnail, spend, reach, CTR, CPC, conversions
