Questions wholesalers ask before switching

Do I have to move my ads or my CRM data?

No. Antigravity connects to your existing Meta and Google ad accounts through their official APIs and reads history in place. Contacts and deals can be imported from CSV with dedupe, or kept in sync through the lead-intake webhook.

Will the AI text sellers something embarrassing?

Every rule has a simulator, so you run the exact conversation against a test thread before it goes live. You set the goal, the tone, the maximum number of replies and the handoff conditions. STOP and wrong-number messages are caught by rules, not by the model, and flag the contact as Do Not Contact instantly.

Do I need to be a marketer to use the ads part?

That is the point. Playbooks encode targeting, copy and creatives for the common seller situations, and the weekly digest talks in outcomes: leads, appointments, contracts. The technical reason sits in brackets for when you want it.

Whose phone numbers are used for calls and texts?

Yours. Numbers are provisioned in your workspace through Telnyx, registered for A2P 10DLC under your brand, and stay with you. Calls, recordings and transcripts are stored in your account.

How is cost per deal calculated?

Each lead keeps the campaign and ad it came from. When the deal closes you record the assignment fee on the deal card, and Insights attributes spend, contracts and fees back to the campaign, creative and platform.

What does it cost beyond the subscription?

Call minutes above your plan, outbound SMS, skip traces above the included quota and AI creative generation are metered and deducted from a prepaid balance. You can see the balance at any time, set auto-recharge, and you get alerts at 80% and 100% of included usage.

Can my team see each other’s contacts?

A regular user sees their own contacts. A project admin sees every contact in the project. Projects are isolated from each other at the database layer, so two markets never mix.

What about compliance?

SMS goes out only from 10DLC-registered numbers. Opt-outs are detected by rules and flag DNC everywhere, including blasts and automations. The deliverability coach scores every text before it goes. Send windows respect the contact’s timezone.
