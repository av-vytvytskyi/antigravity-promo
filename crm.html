Acquisition and disposition pipelines, side by side.

Drag deals between stages on a Kanban board or switch to a table. Several funnels with their own stages, sorting by comp price, date added, last update or open tasks. A score on every card says which deals deserve the next hour.

  • Create a deal with Smarty address autocomplete, export to CSV
  • Milestones strip: lead created plus every stage change, dated from the activity log
  • Stage changes fire automations and feed the velocity analytics

Property intelligence without leaving the deal.

RealEstateAPI data is pulled on creation and can be retried any time. The Property Intelligence card shows what an acquisitions manager checks first. The full drawer shows everything, down to the raw record.

  • Owner on record, mailing address, occupancy, assessed value, open mortgage balance, equity, est. monthly payment, owned-for, last sale
  • Distress flags: pre-foreclosure, auction, tax delinquency, listing failed or cancelled, vacancy
  • Skip trace the owner: phones and emails in one click, within the project quota
  • Market snapshot by zip and recent cash buyers in the zip, each buyer skip-traceable and saveable as a contact

Dispo blast to your buyers list

Audience is every buyer contact or a chosen segment. DNC scrubbed, phones deduped, delivered at a carrier-safe 30 messages a minute through the scheduled worker. Replies land in the inbox with intent labels.

Contracts signed from the deal card

Pick a DocuSeal template, pick the signer among the deal’s contacts, review the prefilled seller name, address and price, send. Status moves sent → viewed → signed and the PDF streams back into the deal. Build templates in an embedded builder, scoped to your project.

Offers with memory

Every offer with amount, method and date. A new offer auto-counters the previous pending one.

Deal economics

ARV, asking, repairs, MAO and spread on one strip, synced with the editable fields below.

Unified timeline

Calls, SMS, emails, tasks, notes, stage changes and contract events in one filterable feed.

A contact list that stays clean while it grows.

Server-side table with filters for type, owner, city, state, created date, has deals, has phone, has email, DNC only. Saved smart segments update themselves as contacts match. Import a CSV and duplicates are matched by phone or email, never created twice.

  • Merge two contacts: deals, conversations and segments move to the survivor, empty fields are backfilled
  • Do-Not-Call flag excludes a contact from blasts and sequences everywhere
  • Tags, column chooser, bulk set type, assign owner, export selection
  • Public lead-intake webhook for PPC forms, landing pages, marketplaces and Zapier, deduped on arrival
