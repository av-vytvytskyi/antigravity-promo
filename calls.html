The call lands in the deal, not in your notebook.

Each call is a card on the deal timeline: recording, transcript, summary with key points and next steps, and the suggestions it produced. Apply an address and the property is re-enriched on the spot: owner, comps, Street View.

  • Suggestions map to real CRM fields: asking price → comp price, callback time → high-priority task
  • Summaries are editable. Your notes win over the model’s
  • Calls also appear in the Inbox thread with the same contact, so SMS and voice read as one conversation

Your numbers, your brand, carrier-compliant.

Buy local numbers by area code, assign them to teammates, set a default caller ID per project. Register your brand and campaign for A2P 10DLC from inside the app and watch the carrier status until it is approved.

  • Browser WebRTC softphone: mute, hold, hang up, floating widget that follows you across pages
  • Caller-ID resolution picks the right project number per user and deal
  • Answering-machine detection, missed call → instant AI text-back
  • Per-second billing beyond included minutes, prepaid credits, auto-recharge

Built for the person who makes forty calls a day.

The floating widget keeps the call alive while you move between the deal, the inbox and the buyers list. The suggestions panel fills in as the seller talks, so the notes are done when the call ends.

  • Usage tab: included minutes, used and remaining, per-user breakdown, full call history with CSV export
  • Credits tab: append-only ledger, balance, top-up, auto-recharge threshold
  • Real-time events over websockets: call updated, completed, summary ready, suggestions updated
