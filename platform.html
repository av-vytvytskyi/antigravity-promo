How a seller moves through the system

The same record, from first click to signed assignment.

Ad → lead

A playbook campaign runs on Meta and Google. The form submission hits the lead-intake endpoint, the contact and deal are created with the campaign attached.

Text → qualified

The ISA replies in seconds, asks for address, condition, price and timing, and hands off with a call task when the seller is hot.

Call → fields

You call from the deal. Transcript, summary and extracted fields land on the card. The address triggers enrichment.

Deal → intel

Owner, mortgage, equity, comps, Street View. Skip trace the owner, pull buyers in the zip, log offers.

Contract → dispo

Send the assignment for e-sign from the card. Stage change fires the dispo blast to your buyers list.

Fee → insight

Enter the assignment fee. Insights attributes it to the campaign and creative, and the next digest scales what worked.

Workspace, team and billing

Projects

Every market or brand is a project with its own accounts, numbers, contacts and settings. Switch from the sidebar. Data is isolated per project at the database layer.

Team & roles

Invite users as member or admin per project. Admins see all contacts, buy and release numbers, and manage billing. Tasks can only be changed by their creator, assignee, watcher or an admin.

Real time

New messages, call events, enrichment progress, generation status and usage alerts are pushed over websockets. Presence shows who is on a thread.

Billing

Stripe subscriptions with plan swap, cancel and resume. Prepaid credits for minutes, SMS, skip traces and AI creatives, with auto-recharge and alerts.

Search

⌘K global search across deals, contacts, ads and creatives. A Copilot panel answers questions about your own data.

Integrations

Meta Marketing API, Google Ads API incl. MCC, Gmail, Outlook, Messenger, Instagram, Telnyx, RealEstateAPI, Smarty, Google Maps, DocuSeal, Stripe, Porkbun and NameSilo, Firecrawl.
