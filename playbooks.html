Playbooks

Start from the five reasons people sell a house for cash.

A playbook pre-fills the campaign priority and pace, generates matching creatives and copy, writes the qualifying lead form and sets the ISA’s questions. You change what you want and launch.

Inherited house

Heirs who live elsewhere and want it handled. Angle: we handle everything, no cleanout, no repairs, close on your timeline. Creatives: the quiet house, the key handover. ISA asks: address, occupancy, who is on title, timeline.

Tired landlord

Rentals with problem tenants or deferred maintenance. Angle: sell with tenants in place, no showings, no evictions. Creatives: the late-night call, the repair invoice. ISA asks: rent, lease status, condition, why now.

Needs repairs

Houses too rough to list. Angle: as-is, we cover repairs, no inspections to fail. ISA asks: roof, foundation, systems, vacancy.

Behind on payments

Owners racing a foreclosure clock. Angle: stop the auction, walk away with cash, fast. ISA asks: lender, months behind, sale date, balance.

Fast cash sale

Life changes that cannot wait for a listing. Angle: offer in 24 hours, close in 7 days. ISA asks: timeline, price expectation, occupancy.

What a playbook sets up for you

It is a starting point, not a cage. Every field stays editable in the wizard.

  • Campaign priority and pace on Smart Campaign step one
  • Four creatives in feed, story, portrait and landscape, generated on step three with matching copy
  • Headline overlay, concept and pain taken from the playbook’s idea
  • Lead form with qualifying questions that match the ISA’s checklist
  • Targeting defaults per platform, your saved locations
  • An autoreply rule you can switch on with the same qualification goal

Follow-up playbooks for Automations

Sequences the engine protects with stop-on-reply, send windows and a daily cap.

Speed to lead

New lead → SMS in 20 seconds → wait 10 minutes → no reply? second SMS with a question → create a call task for the rep on duty.

Missed call text-back

Missed call → “Sorry we missed you, is now a good time?” → add to Callback segment → task for tomorrow morning.

Appointment reminder

Stage = Appointment set → SMS the day before inside the send window → email with the address and what to have ready.

Under contract → dispo

Stage = Under contract → fee ≥ $10k? → dispo blast to cash buyers in the zip and send the assignment for e-sign.

Long-term nurture

Stage = Not now → every 14 days for 90 days, one message a day max, stops the moment the seller replies.

Buyer onboarding

New contact of type buyer → welcome SMS → add to the buyers segment → task to verify proof of funds.
